RAGFramework Employee Policy
============================

Document ID: EMP-POL-001
Domain: Employee Policies
Knowledge Base: employee_knowledge_base
Classification: INTERNAL
Department: HR

1. Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward to the following
calendar year.

2. Sick Leave
Employees may take sick leave according to company policy and applicable
employment regulations.

3. Work From Home
Employees may work from home subject to manager approval and applicable
team policies. Employees working from home must remain available during
normal working hours.

4. Travel Approval
Employee travel requests must be submitted through the company travel
request process. The employee's reporting manager must approve the request
before travel is booked.

5. Employee Benefits
Employees are eligible for benefits according to their employment category
and applicable company policies.

6. HR Restricted Information
Compensation, employee disciplinary records and other confidential employee
information may only be accessed by specifically authorized HR personnel.
