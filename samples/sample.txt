RAGFramework Employee Knowledge Base
====================================

Document ID: RAG-EMP-001
Document Version: 1.0
Department: Human Resources
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Annual Leave Policy
----------------------

Employees are entitled to 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave should normally be requested at least five working days
before the planned start date. Managers are responsible for reviewing
leave requests and approving them based on business requirements.

Employees may carry forward a maximum of 10 unused annual leave days
from one calendar year to the next. Any unused days above the
10-day carry-forward limit will normally expire at the end of the year.

New employees joining during the year receive annual leave on a
pro-rated basis according to their joining date.

Annual leave cannot normally be converted into cash while an employee
remains employed by the company.

2. Sick Leave
-------------

Employees who are unable to work because of illness should notify
their manager before the normal working start time on the first day
of absence.

Employees may take up to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

For an absence lasting more than three consecutive working days,
the employee must provide a medical certificate to Human Resources.

Sick leave is intended for genuine illness and should not be used as
a substitute for annual leave.

3. Work From Home Policy
------------------------

Employees may work from home up to three days per week, subject to
manager approval and team requirements.

Work-from-home requests should normally be submitted at least
one working day in advance.

Employees working from home are expected to remain available during
their normal working hours and attend scheduled meetings.

Certain roles that require physical presence at a company location
may not be eligible for the standard work-from-home arrangement.

4. Core Working Hours
---------------------

The standard working day is eight hours.

Core working hours are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Employees are expected to be available during core working hours
unless they are on approved leave or have an approved alternative
working arrangement.

Teams may establish additional collaboration hours when required
for project delivery or customer commitments.

5. Parental Leave
-----------------

Employees who become parents are eligible for parental leave according
to the applicable company policy and local employment regulations.

Employees should contact Human Resources as early as possible when
planning parental leave.

The exact duration of parental leave depends on the type of leave,
employee eligibility, and applicable local regulations.

Parental leave requests should be submitted through the company's
HR system.

6. Expense Reimbursement
------------------------

Employees may claim reimbursement for approved business expenses.

Expense claims must normally be submitted within 30 calendar days
from the date of the expense.

Receipts are required for individual expenses above INR 1,000.

Travel expenses must comply with the company's travel and expense
policy.

Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving expense claims
before reimbursement.

Personal expenses that are not related to approved business activities
are not eligible for reimbursement.

7. Business Travel
------------------

Employees travelling for business purposes should obtain manager
approval before booking travel.

Flights and hotels should normally be booked through the company's
approved travel platform.

Economy-class air travel is the standard option for journeys shorter
than six hours.

Business-class travel may be approved for longer journeys when allowed
by the applicable travel policy.

Employees should retain receipts and supporting documentation for
business travel expenses.

8. Information Security
-----------------------

Employees must protect company information and must not share
confidential company data with unauthorized individuals.

Company passwords must not be shared with colleagues, customers,
vendors, or other third parties.

Employees must use multi-factor authentication when accessing systems
that require it.

Company laptops must be protected with the required security controls
and should not be left unattended in public places.

Security incidents or suspected data breaches must be reported to the
Information Security team immediately.

9. Password Policy
------------------

Employees must use strong passwords and must not reuse company
passwords for personal services.

Passwords must not be stored in plain-text documents or shared through
email or chat.

Where password managers are approved by the company, employees should
use the company-approved password manager.

Multi-factor authentication must be enabled for systems that support
it.

10. Remote Access
-----------------

Employees accessing company systems from outside the corporate network
must use the approved secure access mechanism.

Unauthorized remote-access software must not be installed on company
devices.

Employees must not connect company devices to untrusted public
networks without using the required security controls.

Remote access activity may be logged and monitored for security and
compliance purposes.

11. Performance Reviews
-----------------------

Employees normally participate in a formal performance review once
per year.

Managers are expected to provide regular feedback throughout the year
and should not wait until the annual review to discuss performance
issues.

Performance discussions should include accomplishments, development
areas, goals, and future responsibilities.

Employees may discuss career development and training requirements
with their manager during the performance review process.

12. Learning and Development
----------------------------

Employees may request training related to their current role or
future career responsibilities.

Training requests should be discussed with the employee's manager
before registration.

The company may reimburse approved training costs when the training
is relevant to business requirements and has received the required
approval.

Certification reimbursement is subject to the applicable learning
and development policy.

13. Employee Onboarding
-----------------------

New employees must complete the required onboarding activities during
their first two weeks.

Onboarding normally includes identity verification, system access,
security training, company orientation, and role-specific training.

Managers are responsible for ensuring that new employees receive the
access and information required to perform their role.

Employees should contact their manager or Human Resources if required
onboarding activities are incomplete.

14. Data Classification
-----------------------

Company information should be handled according to its sensitivity.

Public information may be shared externally when appropriate.

Internal information is intended primarily for employees and approved
business partners.

Confidential information must only be shared with authorized
individuals who have a legitimate business need.

Restricted information requires additional protection and may only be
accessed by specifically authorized personnel.

15. Incident Reporting
----------------------

Employees should report security incidents, suspected phishing
messages, lost company devices, and suspected unauthorized access
immediately.

Incident reports should contain enough information for the security
team to understand what happened, when it happened, and which systems
or information may have been affected.

Employees should not attempt to investigate or delete evidence of a
suspected security incident unless instructed by the Security team.

16. Important Contacts
----------------------

Human Resources handles questions related to leave, benefits,
onboarding, performance reviews, and employee development.

The Information Security team handles security incidents, phishing
reports, unauthorized access, and data protection concerns.

Managers handle day-to-day approvals including annual leave,
work-from-home requests, business travel, and employee development
requests.

Employees should use the official company systems and approved
communication channels when submitting requests or reporting issues.
