RAGFramework Finance Policy
===========================

Document ID: FIN-POL-001
Domain: Finance Policies
Knowledge Base: finance_knowledge_base
Classification: INTERNAL
Department: Finance

1. Business Travel Expenses
Employees may claim eligible business travel expenses according to the
company expense policy.

2. Travel Expense Approval
Travel expenses must be submitted through the expense management system.
Expenses above the applicable approval threshold require Finance Manager
approval.

3. Reimbursement
Approved business expenses are reimbursed through the company's standard
expense processing cycle.

4. Expense Documentation
Employees must provide appropriate receipts and supporting documentation
for reimbursable expenses.

5. Financial Controls
Finance personnel are responsible for reviewing expenses for compliance
with company financial controls.

6. Restricted Financial Information
Financial forecasts, budgets and other restricted financial information may
only be accessed by specifically authorized Finance personnel.
